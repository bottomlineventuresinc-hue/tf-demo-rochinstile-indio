Scope of work

What we do, and how long it usually takes.

Typical time on site for a standard job. Your written quote gives the schedule for your job specifically.

  • Floor tile and marble2-4 days
  • Shower tile3-5 days
  • Backsplashes1-2 days

What we will not do

  • Set tile over a surface that has not been checked for flat and solid.
  • Quote tile from photos alone.
  • Hide material costs inside a vague line item.
  • Start before the layout and the scope, including what comes out first, are agreed.
  • Leave scraps, packaging, or dust behind.

The paperwork

What the written quote covers.

One page, line by line, before any work starts. If something is not on the list, it is named in the exclusions on the same page.

  1. 01The work, described in plain words
  2. 02Areas and square footage, measured
  3. 03Tile or marble named by product, size, and color
  4. 04Surface prep spelled out
  5. 05Removal and haul-away of old tile, or a note that it is excluded
  6. 06Layout, edges, and transitions listed
  7. 07Anything that could change the price, flagged up front
  8. 08What is not included, in writing
